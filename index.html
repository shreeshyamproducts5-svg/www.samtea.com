Ek Perfect Cup Chai Kaise Banayein?

Humari chai 100% natural pattiyon se bani hai. Iska asali swaad lene ke liye in aasan steps ko follow karein:

1. Paani Ubaalein

Ek bartan mein 1 cup paani lein aur use achhi tarah ubalne dein.

2. Chai Patti Daalein

Ubalte hue paani mein 1 chammach Sam🧿 Premium Tea daalein aur 2 minute tak ubaalein.

3. Doodh aur Cheeni

Apne swaad ke anusar doodh aur cheeni milayein. Ise 1-2 minute aur pakne dein.

4. Chhane aur Piyein

Garma garam chai ko cup mein chhan lein aur uske kadak swaad ka maza lein.
